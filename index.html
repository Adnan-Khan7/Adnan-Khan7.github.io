About

Welcome. I'm Adnan Khan — from Pakistan, nurtured in the city of flowers, Peshawar. I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Carleton University's Intelligent Machines Lab, in Ottawa, Canada. Proudly both a tea and a coffee person.

My research focuses on navigating the responsible use of AI — specifically generative AI — for social good. I work on adapting generative vision-language models under domain shifts for real-world applications in accessibility and healthcare.

I'm also active in teaching, mentorship, and student-led community initiatives.

Selected work

TactileEval

IEEE SMC 2026

A step towards automating expert validation of tactile graphics: a five-category quality taxonomy aligned with BANA standards, 14,095 crowd-sourced annotations across 66 object classes, and a ViT-guided pipeline that edits the defects it detects.

Paper · Project page · Code · Dataset · Talk

TactileNet

IEEE SMC 2025

The first comprehensive dataset and AI framework for generating embossing-ready tactile graphics, fine-tuning Stable Diffusion with LoRA and DreamBooth. Expert evaluation found 92.86% adherence to accessibility standards.

Paper · Project page · Dataset · Talk
